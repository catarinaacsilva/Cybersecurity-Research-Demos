How it works The drawn path becomes a 1 Hz trace with phone-like GPS error. Movement features over 20 s windows (speed, turning, spread, dwell) feed an autoencoder trained on routine walks: a high reconstruction error is a possible crisis. A small neural agent, trained by neuro-evolution, turns that score into the membrane radius (5–200 m). Each released point is shifted by planar-Laplace noise (geo-indistinguishability) with mean offset equal to the radius. Routine walks for training follow the real Aveiro street network (OpenStreetMap); crises (pacing, freezing, wandering) are injected, as in the paper.
