How it works

Categorization: each field's name and real values are compared with short definitions of the three categories (character n-gram TF-IDF); the closest wins. Agreement with an expert: –.

Dynamic tags: a profiler is retrained on the collected fields to guess each sensitive fact not collected (bar; the line marks guessing), and the share of people made unique is counted.

The score Privacy value = structural × (½ direct + ½ dynamic). Direct: collected fields weighted by category (sensitive 1, identity 0.6, socio-economic 0.35) and by how much sensitive information their values carry (mutual information). Dynamic: the strongest tag. Structural: mean of frequency and retention; coarse granularity bins the values, which changes the measured tags. This aggregation is the demo's simplification of PsDC's privacy value.
